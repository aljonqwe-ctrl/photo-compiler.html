On quality: every photo is drawn at its full source resolution, letterboxed (not cropped or stretched) to fit the 1920×1080 frame — nothing is resampled below its original detail. Export always produces MP4 (H.264), on every browser, using an in-browser encoder: each frame is captured as a lossless PNG first, then encoded at CRF 0 (H.264's mathematically lossless mode) in 4:4:4 chroma (no color subsampling), so no color — from the letterbox bars to the photos themselves — is degraded. The trade-off is file size (lossless video is large) and encode time; a status bar at the bottom of the page shows exactly what stage it's at. The source files on your device are never altered.
